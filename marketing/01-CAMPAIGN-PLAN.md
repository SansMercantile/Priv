# 01 — Campaign plan

**Product:** Priv — AI trading terminal by Sans Mercantile
**Primary URL:** `https://priv.sansmercantile.com`

---

## 1. Positioning

> **Precision entries, read from the market — not guessed at.**

Priv is not another signal newsletter and not a bot you hand your money to. It
is a terminal that reads price action, argues about it with five agents, and
hands you a *complete* trade — entry, two targets, a stop that trails to
breakeven — while your funds never leave your own broker.

The three words the campaign must own, in order:

1. **Complete** — every setup arrives finished. No "watch this level".
2. **Provable** — Strategy Lab backtests on real candles before you risk a cent.
3. **Yours** — official OAuth to *your* broker. Funds never leave it.

## 2. Audience

| Segment | What they already do | The wedge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Retail discretionary traders** (25–45) | Charts at night, Discord signals, manual orders | complete setup, delivered, on every screen |
| **Prop-firm / funded traders** | Risk rules, drawdown limits, consistency targets | event setups, prop-firm params, Sovereign tier |
| **Systematic tinkerers** | Script ideas they never test | Strategy Lab, 152 runs, reproducible params |
| **"Set and forget" accounts** | Worry about missing moves | 28-second autonomous cycle, fail-closed |

Anti-persona: people looking for guaranteed returns or a hands-off money
machine. Nothing in this campaign promises outcomes — only process.

## 5. Cadence

- **Week 1:** 1 hero cut + 3 feed posts (A, B, F).
- **Week 2:** explainer live on the landing page; 3 more feed posts (C, D, E);
  story rotation begins.
- **Weeks 3–4:** retargeting with `thumb-*` and the 6 s bumper; two headline
  variants tested against each other.
- **Weeks 5–6:** refresh stat numbers from live product data; re-render; re-publish.

Cadence assumption: the LinkedIn content engine already publishes on the
12 h / 8 h schedules listed in `00-ROADMAP.md`. These assets are the visual
layer on top of that stream, not a replacement for it.

## 6. KPIs

| Stage | Metric | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attention | 3 s view rate (video), thumb-stop (static) | > 30 % |
| Interest | CTR to `priv.sansmercantile.com` | > 1.2 % paid, > 3 % owned |
| Intent | free-plan sign-ups attributable to campaign | weekly cohort up and to the right |
| Conversion | $29+ plan starts | tracked at the pricing card |
| Retention | day-7 activation (first signal opened) | product-side, not asset-side |

## 7. Guardrails

- **No performance claims.** "Prove it before you risk it" is about *their*
  testing, not our results.
- **No lifestyle imagery.** The art is systems, glass, paper, city — the
  product is the hero.
- **Prices are a single source of truth.** Live tiers are Free $0, Pro $29,
  Elite $79, Sovereign $149, Autonomous $199. They appear in `04-COPY-BANK.md`,
  `poster_specs.py`, and the pricing poster — change all three together.
- **The carnation is the mark.** Red flower, gold ring, `PRIV` wordmark, URL
  right — `brand.footer()` draws it on every asset. Never restyle it locally.
